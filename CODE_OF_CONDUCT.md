# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Falcon OSINT Framework is a professional cybersecurity project. We are committed
to a respectful, inclusive, and safety-conscious community for investigators,
researchers, developers, educators, and maintainers.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate with professionalism and good faith.
- Respect differing levels of experience and investigative background.
- Give and receive technical feedback constructively.
- Protect sensitive information, including personal data and investigation
  details.
- Discuss offensive security, OSINT, and investigative techniques in a lawful and
  ethical context.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Encouraging stalking, doxxing, credential theft, unauthorized access, or abuse
  of OSINT techniques.
- Sharing exploit instructions or datasets in a way that creates unreasonable
  risk to real people or organizations.
- Repeated disruption of issues, pull requests, discussions, or maintainer work.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when behavior violates this Code of Conduct. Enforcement decisions
will consider user safety, project integrity, and the seriousness of the conduct.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through the repository's maintainer contact channel. If a
report involves a security vulnerability, follow `SECURITY.md` instead.

Reports should include relevant links, screenshots, dates, and context when safe
to share. Maintainers will handle reports with discretion and avoid unnecessary
disclosure.
